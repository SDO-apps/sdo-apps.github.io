Pillars

Sleep, food, and workouts in one place. Pillars brings in your Apple Watch data, lets you log meals and symptoms, and helps you see how your habits connect.

  • On your iPhone. No account and no servers; your data stays on the device.
  • On-device insights. Patterns are found with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, not sent to an AI company.
  • Read-only Health. Pillars reads Apple Health and never writes to it.

About
