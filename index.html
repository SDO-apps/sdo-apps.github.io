About

Small beginnings.

Afield Studio is an independent studio started by Sebastian Olmos to explore ideas and turn the useful ones into real products.

Most things worth making start small: a question, a rough first version, and the habit of coming back to improve it. Each product here began with a specific, everyday problem, like making sense of your sleep, setting a lineup, or tailoring a resume, and tries to solve it simply and honestly.

  • Curiosity over assumptions

    Start with a real question and let what you learn shape the product.

  • Clarity over complexity

    A clear purpose, a clean interface, nothing that doesn't earn its place.

  • Consistency over comparison

    Keep showing up. Steady improvement beats a big launch.

  • Useful over hype

    Measure success by whether it helps someone, not by how it sounds.
