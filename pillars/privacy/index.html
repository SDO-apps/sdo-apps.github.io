Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Pillars is an iOS app by Afield Studio.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

This page describes what Pillars does with your information.

At a glance

  • Your data stays on your iPhone. Pillars has no servers and no account. The only things that leave the phone are food searches (to USDA) and workout place searches (to Apple Maps), described below.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your information. We never receive it.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and there are no analytics or ads.
  • Apple Health is read-only. Pillars never writes to Health.
  • Insights use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to an AI company.

What Pillars is

Pillars shows the workouts and sleep you already track with Apple Fitness and Apple Health, lets you keep a food and symptom diary, and points out patterns across sleep, food, and exercise.

It is not a medical device, not diagnostic, and not a substitute for professional care. Insights are patterns in your own data, not medical findings or advice.

Data stored on your device

Pillars stores on this device only:

  • Meals, symptoms, and favorite meals you log, including each food's portion and its calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber
  • Which Apple Fitness workouts you chose to show in Pillars
  • Names and places you give your workouts (Apple Health itself is not changed)
  • Insights it generated for you
  • The first name you give for your greeting, and app settings (for example appearance)

This data is encrypted by iOS and unreadable while your iPhone is locked. It is not synced to iCloud by Pillars. It may be included in your own iPhone backups; turn on Apple's Advanced Data Protection to end-to-end encrypt iCloud backups.

You can delete individual logs, or erase everything in Settings → Delete All Pillars Data. Deleting the app also removes it. Data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, Pillars reads (and never writes) from Apple Health:

  • Workouts, including routes, active and resting energy, distance, steps, power, cadence, and effort
  • Sleep analysis and stages
  • Heart: heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability
  • Overnight vitals: blood oxygen, respiratory rate, wrist temperature, and breathing disturbances
  • Fitness: cardio fitness (VO₂ max), heart rate recovery, Exercise minutes, and time in daylight

This is used only to show you your own data and patterns inside the app. Pillars reads it from Health when you use the app and does not keep its own copy. You can turn access off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

HealthKit data is never used for marketing, advertising, or tracking, and never sold. It stays on your device, with one exception you control: if you tap Use where this workout started, the starting point of that workout's route is sent to Apple Maps to look up a place name (see below).

On-device AI

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Pillars uses Apple's on-device model to choose and title the most relevant patterns for you. This runs entirely on your iPhone; your data is not sent to Apple, the developer, or any AI provider. The patterns and numbers themselves are calculated by the app, not written by AI.

Food search (USDA)

Common foods are built into the app, so most searches never leave your iPhone. For branded products or foods the built-in list doesn't have, Pillars sends only the search text you type, or a food's USDA ID number to fetch its nutrients, to USDA FoodData Central (a public U.S. government service). It never sends your food diary or Health data. USDA's own terms apply to that service.

Food photos and descriptions

If you scan or pick a photo of a meal, or describe a meal in words, Pillars identifies the foods on your iPhone (Apple's on-device image recognition and Apple Intelligence). Photos are not saved and are never uploaded. Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a meal.

Workout places (Apple Maps)

You can give a workout a place, like a gym or park. When you search for one, Pillars sends only the text you type to Apple Maps to suggest places. If you tap Use where this workout started, it sends that workout route's starting point to Apple Maps to find a place name; this happens only when you tap it. No heart rate, calories, or other health values are sent. The place you pick is saved on your iPhone. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple Maps.

Exports

If you export a health report (for example to share with a doctor), you choose what it includes: sleep, workouts, and/or food and symptoms. The PDF is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to send it. Pillars deletes its temporary copy after sharing.

Tracking, ads, and sale of data

  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No sale or sharing of personal information

California residents: we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use HealthKit data for marketing.

Children

Pillars is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
